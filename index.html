Our story

Rooted in legacy, driven by love.

Multida's Daughters' Enterprise honors my late mother, Multida, a Haitian entrepreneur whose creativity, generosity, and love for her community inspire everything I do.

From creating floral arrangements to baking homemade Haitian patties, she brought people together through her talents.

Today, I carry her spirit forward, providing tables, chairs, and linens to help you celebrate life's special moments. With my sister bringing her decorating talents, we serve Miami with care, heart, and a personal touch.

Rentals are for 24 hours unless a different duration is arranged in advance.

Yes, we offer delivery and pickup services within Miami and surrounding areas. Delivery fees apply based on distance and order size.
