Terms & Conditions

Welcome to Multida's Daughters' Enterprise. By accessing our website and using our rental services, you agree to the following terms and conditions:

1. Rental Eligibility

You must be 18 years or older to rent from our company. A valid ID may be required at pickup or delivery.

2. Payment Terms

Full payment is required at the time of booking to confirm your reservation. We accept secure online payments via Stripe and PayPal.

3. Reservation Changes & Cancellations

You may cancel or modify your order up to 72 hours before your event for a full refund. Orders canceled within 72 hours are subject to a 50% cancellation fee.

4. Delivery & Pickup

Deliveries are curbside only unless otherwise arranged. You must be present at the scheduled time. Late pickups or inaccessible delivery areas may incur additional fees.

5. Equipment Care

All rental items must be returned in the same condition they were delivered. Damage, loss, or missing items will be billed at replacement value.

6. Liability Disclaimer

Multida's Daughters' Enterprise is not liable for injuries or property damage resulting from misuse of our rental equipment.

7. Weather Policy

We are not responsible for weather-related cancellations. We recommend placing linens and equipment indoors during rain or strong wind.

8. Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Florida.

Rental Agreement

This Rental Agreement ("Agreement") is made between the Renter and Multida's Daughters' Enterprise. It covers the renter's information (name, phone, event date and delivery/pickup location) and the rental items booked: tables, chairs, cocktail tables and linens.

Payment Terms

100% due at booking. Non-refundable if canceled within 72 hours of delivery. Renter agrees to be charged for lost or damaged items.

Rental Period

24 hours unless otherwise agreed upon in writing.

Security Deposit

A refundable deposit of $50 may be charged and returned upon inspection of all items.

Care & Use

Renter agrees to handle all rental items with care and keep them protected from weather, misuse, or damage.

Signatures

The agreement is signed and dated by the Renter and a Company Representative.

Privacy Policy

How We Use Your Information

  • To contact you regarding your booking or follow-up

Your Rights
