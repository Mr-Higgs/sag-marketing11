Our story

A mother's heart, carried forward.

This business is inspired by my late mother, Multida, a woman of courage, vision, and compassion.

Born in Haiti, she was an entrepreneur at heart. She ran businesses back home and, after migrating to the United States, never stopped dreaming of owning more. I remember vividly the days she spent taking floral arrangement courses and catering classes, preparing herself to serve others with grace.

Whether she was decorating weddings, catering community events, or baking Haitian patties from scratch to sell, she did it all with love and purpose. She opened her doors to others, always helping, always creating. Her heart was her business.

And now, I carry that same heart into Multida's Daughters. I hope she is proud as I continue the work she began.

Honoring her legacy and drawing from the gifts she passed down, we promise to make every occasion fabulous and favorable, because your joy is our purpose.
