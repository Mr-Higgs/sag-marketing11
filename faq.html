Home/FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about renting with us. Can't find your answer? Give us a call, we're happy to help.

How long is the rental period?

Rentals are for 24 hours unless a different duration is arranged in advance.

Do you deliver and pick up?

Yes, we offer delivery and pickup services within Miami and surrounding areas. Delivery fees apply based on distance and order size.

How do I pay for my rental?

You can pay securely online using PayPal, Stripe, or credit/debit card at checkout.

What happens if an item is damaged?

Customers are responsible for any lost or damaged items and will be billed at replacement cost.

Can I cancel or change my order?

Yes! Cancellations made 72+ hours in advance receive a full refund. Changes are allowed based on availability. See our refund policy.

Do you offer same-day rentals?

Depending on availability, same-day rentals may be accommodated. Call us directly at (786) 763-3545 for urgent requests.

Can your company set up my space with tables and chairs?

Yes, we offer full setup services for your event.

  • $75 per 50 chairs
  • $5 per table

Teardown services are also available at the same rates. Let us handle both setup and cleanup so you can enjoy your event stress-free.

Is your website accessible to people with disabilities?

Yes, our website is built with accessibility in mind, including keyboard navigation, readable contrast and screen-reader friendly markup.
